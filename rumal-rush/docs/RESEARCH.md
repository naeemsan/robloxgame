# Why this game

Rumal Rush adapts Rumal Jhapatta / Steal the Bacon: an attacker must choose when to commit to a central pickup, then escape while the defender pursues. This supplies timing, baiting, route choice and chase decisions without expensive graphics.

Alternatives considered were Pithu/Lagori, Kho Kho and marbles. Pithu offers strong teamwork but needs ball hit validation and rebuilding interactions. Kho Kho adds rule-teaching and chase-transfer complexity. Marbles depends heavily on networked physics and slower aiming. Rumal was selected as the smallest complete competitive loop that can also support a solo practice opponent.

## Observations — 24 October 2026, Asia/Karachi

Read-only queries to Roblox's public omni-search endpoint returned 40 experience entries each for:
- steal the bacon
- rumal
- lagori
- pithu

Most returned entries were unrelated discovery recommendations, including roleplay and generic “steal” games. These responses did not establish a directly comparable implementation or absence of competitors. The Kho Kho request was rate-limited with HTTP 429; it was not retried repeatedly. A Google search page did not provide usable extracted results.

Samples and actual experience URLs returned by the search endpoint are preserved in research-observations.json. They are observations, not endorsements or verified gameplay comparisons. Search ranking, fuzzy matches, unpublished games, alternative spelling and inaccessible pages limit coverage.

No claim is made that this is the first such game on Roblox. The aim is a coherent playable adaptation with clear rules, fair cosmetic-only purchases and low visual complexity. Retention, originality in the full marketplace and earnings remain unproven.
