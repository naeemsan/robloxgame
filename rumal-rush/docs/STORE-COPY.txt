TITLE
Rumal Rush — Courtyard Duels

SHORT DESCRIPTION
One scarf. Two rivals. Grab it and get home — or tag the runner before they escape!

FULL DESCRIPTION
The courtyard is calling.

Rumal Rush brings a real playground chase into quick Roblox duels:
• Bait your rival, grab the rumal, and dash to your colored home line.
• Miss the pickup? Chase the carrier and tag them to score.
• First team to 3 wins.
• Play solo against Milo, or bring friends and rotate through team duels.
• Earn scarf colors and trails just by playing.

Controls:
Move: WASD / thumbstick
Grab: E / gamepad X / touch button
Dash: Shift / gamepad B / touch button

Cosmetics never increase speed or tagging strength.
